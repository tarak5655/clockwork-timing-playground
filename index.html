Model assumptions & source reading

Clock edges & uncertainty

The launch FF rises at 0 and T. First-stage FFs capture at T + capture latency. Positive-level latches open at T/2 + latency and close at T + latency. Next-stage FFs use a 180-degree clock: capture at 1.5T + next latency, previous hold edge at 0.5T + next latency. First-stage hold uses the previous capture/closing edge. All values are in picoseconds.

Setup margin = setup uncertainty + relative inter-cycle jitter. Hold margin = hold uncertainty + relative same-edge jitter. Jitter is already a bound on the launch/capture difference, not per-clock RMS. Correlated common-mode jitter cancels for hold; its default is zero. Enter residual uncertainty to avoid counting jitter twice.

Educational, not signoff STA

Cell delay = base delay × VT factor / sqrt(drive size). HVT 1.24, SVT 1.12, LVT 1.00, ULVT 0.82. Wire minimum = 55% of its maximum. Each delay buffer adds 24/40 ps; each extra branch adds 3/6 ps of launch loading. These are illustrative, not measured library values. Faster/larger cells generally trade area or leakage for speed.

Late/early OCV derates apply to combinational paths. Bounded noise increases max and decreases min delay; minimum is clamped at zero. Buffering here adds delay, not RC repeater optimization. No slew, actual RC, CPPR, clock-tree OCV, metastability probability, pulse-width checks, or multi-cycle exceptions are simulated. Failed first-stage checks make downstream values provisional.

Borrowing disabled means a zero-borrow budget at latch opening; the latch remains physically transparent. Borrowing is capped at its closing setup deadline. Scores cover the displayed setup, hold, and borrow-budget checks only.
