Model assumptions & source reading

Clock edges & uncertainty

Source FF launches at nominal 0. First FF setup is T, hold is 0. A LOW-LEVEL latch has gate HIGH from 0 to T/2 and LOW (active) from T/2 to T: falling edge opens, rising edge closes. Its setup uses closing T; hold uses previous closing 0. Add each endpoint's own latency to every nominal edge.

After a first FF, same-phase second FF setup is 2T and hold is T; the first FF relaunches at T. After a latch, same-phase second FF setup is T and hold is 0 because data passes during transparency. Opposite-phase second FF setup is 1.5T and hold is 0.5T for either source. Phase is independent per branch. Latency = signed offset + clock wire + clock BUF sum; phase is not skew.

Setup margin = setup uncertainty + relative inter-cycle jitter. Hold margin = hold uncertainty + relative same-edge jitter. Jitter is a launch/capture difference bound, not RMS. Do not include it again inside residual uncertainty.

Illustrative cell library

At LVT / 1x, min/max delay: BUF 24/40, AND 30/60, OR 32/64, NAND 20/42, NOR 24/48, NOT 12/25 ps. Every cell scales by VT / sqrt(size): HVT 1.24, SVT 1.12, LVT 1, ULVT 0.82. Clock BUF nominal delay is 32 ps before scaling. No clock OCV or CPPR is modelled.

Data minimum wire delay is 55% of maximum. OCV and noise apply after summing cells and wire; early delay is clamped to zero. Fanout adds 3/6 ps min/max per extra branch. Gates are sensitized timing arcs, not a Boolean simulator or a function-preserving ECO. Reordering cells leaves this additive model unchanged. No slew/load lookup, actual RC, metastability or pulse-width analysis.

Latch transfer is the educational approximation max(open, A) + DQ; opening CQ and transparent DQ are not separate arcs. Intended cycles stay fixed: missed captures are not repaired by cycle re-pairing. Borrowing disabled imposes zero borrow, not an edge-triggered latch. Upstream failures make downstream results provisional. Educational model, not signoff STA.
